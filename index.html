Research

Clinical EEG archive and foundation model

2024 – present Clinical Neuro-Informatics Center, Mount Sinai · PI: Benjamin Kummer

I extracted Mount Sinai's entire clinical EEG archive and put it in a format researchers can use. I'm now using that archive to build a foundation EEG model that can predict epilepsy from an EEG read as "normal."

Auditory temporal processing (PhD)

2018 – 2022 Systems Neuroscience of Auditory Perception Lab, Purdue · PI: Hari Bharadwaj

I studied how the auditory system tracks sound over time, using perceptual testing, EEG, and single-unit recordings. My work focused on temporal processing, auditory binding, and processing differences in neuropsychiatric populations such as autism.

NIH T32 DC016853 · 2 years

Automating deep brain stimulation programming

2014 – 2016 DeWeerth Group, Georgia Tech · PI: Stephen P. DeWeerth

Through computational simulations, I developed a black-box optimization approach to automate and improve how DBS devices are programmed.

NIH Computational Neuroscience Training Grant
